Football Web Simulator is a browser-based football management game where you can take control of your favorite clubs, manage squads, create tactics, and compete for trophies.

Features

Multiple leagues and teams: Choose from major football leagues around the world.
Players from different eras: Build squads featuring footballers from different generations.
Dynamic matches: Matches progress automatically, allowing you to focus on tactics and decisions.
Tactical management: Customize formations, playing styles, and substitutions during matches.
Club management: Make decisions that shape your team's performance and future.
Play your way: Recreate historic teams, build dream squads, or lead an underdog to glory.

Goal

Build your legacy, dominate the competition, and create your own football history.

How to Play

Open the game in your web browser, select a team, configure your tactics, and start your career.
